Our Story

From Tucker, Georgia - to retailers nationwide.

Empire Distributors Group was founded on a simple idea: independent retailers deserve direct access to premium brands, at fair wholesale pricing, with the kind of service that only comes from a team that picks up the phone.

We source directly from trusted manufacturers, stock deep across vape, kratom, delta, mushroom, blue lotus, and wellness supplements, and ship fast with the top freight carriers - reaching even the most remote corners of the United States.

Every product is vetted. Every order is fulfilled quickly. When you need us, you reach a real person on WhatsApp - not a ticket queue.
